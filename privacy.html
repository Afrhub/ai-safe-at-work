Privacy

Privacy.

A product about handling data responsibly should be plain about its own. This notice says what Attest AI holds, why, and for how long. Last updated 9 Sep 2026.

Who we are

Attest AI is the trading name for the AI Safe@Work course and Governance Centre at attest-ai.com. For anything on this page, write to hello@attest-ai.com. Attest AI is the controller for the data described below, except where your employer has bought the service and asked us to process staff records on its behalf, in which case your employer is the controller and we act on its instructions.

Reading the public site and the free module

No account, no analytics, no advertising pixel. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google sees the IP address that requested them and nothing more. Block Google Fonts in your browser and the pages fall back to system fonts.

If you have an account

We hold your email address, your name if you enter one, your role (staff member, manager or partner), which manager's team you belong to, your authenticator enrolment, and sign-in events. We hold this because you or your employer asked for an account; the lawful basis is the contract with you or your employer. Your password is never visible to us; sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, hosted in London.

Training and governance records

When you pass a module's knowledge check we record the module, the score and the date. When you acknowledge a policy we record which document and when. Managers see these records for the staff on their team; that is the point of the product, so an employer can show regulators and auditors that staff are trained. Managers' governance registers (risks, incidents, tools, use cases and the document pack) are stored against the manager's account and visible only to that manager and to the staff they publish to.

Buying

Payments are taken by Stripe by Direct Debit. Stripe receives your name, email address, billing address and bank details under its own privacy notice; we never see your bank details. We keep the invoice record and the number of seats bought.

Email

We send sign-in links, password resets, invitations and order confirmations through Resend. We do not send marketing email.

Where it lives and who sees it

Data is stored by Supabase in London (AWS eu-west-2) and served by Netlify. Our processors are Supabase (database and sign-in), Netlify (hosting and forms), Stripe (payments) and Resend (email). We do not sell or share personal data with anyone else, and we do not use it to train AI models.

How long

Account and training records are kept while the account or your employer's contract is active. When a contract ends, or you ask us to close your account, records are deleted within 90 days unless your employer asks to keep training evidence longer, in which case it is kept under their instruction. Payment records are kept for six years as UK tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@attest-ai.com. If your employer is the controller we will pass your request to them and help them answer it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
