Attest has no self-serve sign-up by design — accounts are provisioned: born from a purchase (manager) or an invitation (staff). That's the enterprise-course pattern (Coursera for Business, SANS: seats are granted, never self-registered), with staff on magic links (Slack/Udemy Business pattern) so 25 non-technical employees never hold a password. What to change (in order)

  1. Nothing about the screens — the flow design is already right. Gated sign-up, password+MFA for privileged roles, magic links for staff is exactly the enterprise-course pattern. The struggle to "visualise" it is that the journey only exists as hidden <form hidden> steps inside portal/login.html — this page is the missing map.
  2. Unblock SMTP (after the attest-ai.com domain move). Three of four lanes dead-end in an email that cannot send. Until then, every flow above is theatre.
  3. Say the provisioning rule out loud on the sign-in card. The current copy already does ("Access is set up when you buy a plan or when your manager invites you") — keep it; it is the honest replacement for a Sign up button.
  4. Keep role routing server-side — the session role decides manager/end-user/reseller portal, mirroring win2linux's auth-me rule.
