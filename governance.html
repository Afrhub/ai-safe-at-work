Governance Centre

Your AI governance, in one place.

Registers, risk assessments, vendor due diligence and staff sign-off, kept current and organised the way an auditor asks for them. Part of the Attest AI Platform, reached by signing in to your portal.

Try it now, no sign in → Sign in to your portal →

What is inside

Every section below is driven by records you enter once. Nothing is typed twice, and the policy writes itself from the registers.

Registers

AI Tool Register

Every AI tool your people can reach, including the ones you never bought. Account type is recorded separately, because the same tool on an enterprise account and a personal one are two different answers.

Registers

Use Case Register

What each tool is actually used for, who owns it, and the rating its risk assessment produced.

Risk

AI Risk Register and assessments

Likelihood and impact on a matrix, with residual risk left as "Not scored" until mitigation is planned rather than quietly assuming the inherent score.

Suppliers

Vendor Due Diligence

Structured supplier diligence with the mandatory checks built in, covering data location, retention, training opt-outs, sub-processors, EU AI Act deployer duties and Cyber Resilience Act scope. Send it before you buy.

Suppliers

Vendor Risk Score

Score the answers when they come back and record a go or no-go decision.

Policy

Acceptable Use Policy

Generated from your registers, not typed from a template. Publish it and every member of staff is asked to acknowledge the new version.

Rollout

Staff and policy sign-off

Your roster, who has acknowledged the current policy version, and who has not.

Accountability

Incidents, RACI and Steering Group

Incident reports, a roles matrix and terms of reference, so the question "who was responsible" has a written answer.

Overview

The Governance Centre itself

A list generated from your registers, not typed. Items clear themselves when the underlying record is fixed, so you work top down.

Getting in

The Governance Centre is part of the Attest AI Platform. Managers sign in to the portal to reach it; staff sign in to the course and to acknowledge the policy. If you want to look around first, the demo runs on sample data with no account at all.
