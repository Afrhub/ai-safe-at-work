Who we help.

Whether you are just beginning your AI journey or looking to mature the governance you already have, Attest AI provides the right level of support to help your organisation adopt AI safely, securely and responsibly.

Small businesses Mid-market Growing Regulated Public sector suppliers Professional services Technology & IT

Small businesses

Your staff are already using AI. The question is whether anyone has written down what is allowed, and whether you could show a customer that you have.

Common challenges

  • Staff already using AI tools, informally
  • No AI policy, or one nobody has read
  • Unsure which tools are safe to allow
  • Limited internal governance expertise
  • No dedicated compliance resource

How Attest AI helps

  • AI Safe@Work staff training, with certificates
  • The core governance documents, ready to adapt
  • Practical guidance written for non-specialists
  • An affordable starting point at £990 a year

Mid-market organisations

Adoption has outrun the paperwork. Different departments have chosen different tools, and nobody has a single view of what is in use or who approved it.

Common challenges

  • AI adoption accelerating across the business
  • Different departments using different tools
  • Multiple stakeholders, no single owner
  • Governance becoming inconsistent
  • Customer due diligence questions increasing

How Attest AI helps

  • One central governance platform
  • Live registers and a dashboard of what needs attention
  • Policy management with versioning and staff sign-off
  • Reporting and evidence you can hand to a customer
  • Governance that keeps pace, not a one-off project

Growing organisations

Governance that fitted forty people rarely fits a hundred. Organisations scaling quickly need the same answers to keep working as the organisation changes underneath them.

What changes as you scale

  • New starters every month, each needing training
  • New AI use cases appearing faster than they are recorded
  • New software suppliers to assess
  • Governance requirements evolving with the business

How Attest AI helps

  • Training that new starters complete on joining
  • A use case register that grows with the business
  • Structured supplier due diligence, applied consistently
  • One record of who was trained, and when

Regulated organisations

Financial services, legal, healthcare, manufacturing, professional services and critical infrastructure. Where AI use has to be defensible, not just documented.

Why they come to us

  • Governance evidence, not just governance intent
  • Audit readiness
  • Supplier assurance requests from customers
  • Workforce training that can be evidenced
  • AI risk management that stands up to scrutiny

How Attest AI helps

  • Registers, assessments and evidence in one place
  • Sector overlays for financial services, healthcare and the public sector
  • Mapping to the EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST AI RMF
  • Consultancy where an internal team needs hands-on help

Public sector suppliers

Organisations selling into government and the wider public sector are increasingly asked to show how they use AI, before the contract is awarded rather than after.

What buyers are asking for

  • Evidence of responsible AI use
  • A governance framework you actually follow
  • AI risk management
  • Workforce training records

How Attest AI helps

  • Documented answers to the questions buyers ask
  • Training records you can produce on request
  • A public sector overlay covering the specifics
  • Evidence packs to attach to a tender response

Professional services

Law firms, accountants, architects and consultants. Heavy AI use, and confidential client information in almost every prompt.

Common challenges

  • Client-confidential material going into public tools
  • Professional obligations that predate AI
  • Client contracts that now ask about AI use
  • Fee earners adopting tools faster than the firm can assess them

How Attest AI helps

  • A clear never-paste list your people will follow
  • Training that covers confidentiality, not just prompts
  • Supplier due diligence before a tool reaches client work
  • Evidence for client and insurer questionnaires

Technology and IT providers

Software companies, managed service providers, IT support and cyber firms. Often the people your clients ask first, which is why many of them become partners rather than customers.

Common challenges

  • Clients asking AI governance questions you did not sell
  • AI appearing inside the tools you already manage
  • Security questionnaires now including AI sections
  • No governance offering to put in front of a client

How Attest AI helps

  • Governance you can deliver to your own client base
  • Training and documents that fit an existing support contract
  • A route to become a partner rather than a reseller
