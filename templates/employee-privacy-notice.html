Employee Privacy Notice.

What the organisation holds about the people who work for it, why, who sees it and for how long. UK GDPR Articles 13 and 14 require this to be given to every employee; the Governance dashboard publishes it and records each acknowledgement.

Designed to print clean. Replace every bracketed field. Staff acknowledge this document from their portal once it is live.
Privacy notice

Employee Privacy Notice of [Company Name]

Effective: [Date] · Version: 1.0 · Owner: [Role, e.g. Data Protection Lead / HR Director] · Review cycle: Annual, or on any change in processing.

1. Who we are

[Company Name] ([registered address], ICO registration [ZA number]) is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. Questions go to [Data Protection Lead name / role] at [privacy email].

2. Who this covers

Employees, workers, contractors, apprentices, volunteers and job applicants. Where a section applies only to some of these, it says so.

3. What we hold

  • Identity and contact: name, address, phone, personal email, date of birth, emergency contacts, right-to-work documents.
  • Employment: contract, role, pay, bank details for payroll, National Insurance number, tax code, pension, absence, performance, disciplinary and grievance records, references.
  • Systems: user accounts, access logs, device identifiers, email and file activity to the extent described in the Employee Monitoring Policy.
  • Training: which courses you completed and when, including AI literacy training and policy acknowledgements recorded through the Attest AI platform.
  • Special category data (only where needed): health information for sickness, adjustments and occupational health; equality monitoring you choose to give; trade union membership for check-off; criminal records checks where the role requires them.

4. Why, and on what lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis (Art 6)Special category condition (Art 9), if any
Running your employment: pay, benefits, contract, leaveContractEmployment law obligations (Sch 1 Pt 1 DPA 2018)
Legal duties: tax, right to work, health and safetyLegal obligationEmployment law obligations
Performance, training, AI literacy (EU AI Act Art 4 where applicable)Legitimate interests: running a competent, compliant workforceNone

Health, adjustments, occupational healthLegal obligation / legitimate interestsEmployment law; occupational medicine
Equality monitoringLegitimate interestsEquality of opportunity (Sch 1 para 8), or consent

Where we rely on legitimate interests we have recorded the balancing test in the Lawful Basis Register. You may object; see section 9.

5. Where it comes from

Mostly from you. Also from referees, right-to-work checks, [background check provider], occupational health providers, payroll and HR systems, and the systems you use at work.

6. Who sees it

  • Inside the organisation: HR, your line manager, payroll, IT for account administration, and senior management where a decision needs it.
  • Processors acting on our instructions: [payroll provider], [HR system], [IT / email provider], [pension provider], Attest AI (training records and policy acknowledgements, hosted in the UK).
  • Others where the law requires or permits: HMRC, pension regulators, courts, our insurers and professional advisers, a buyer in a sale of the business.

7. Transfers outside the UK

[None. / We transfer data to X in Y under the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / adequacy regulations.]

8. How long we keep it

  • Recruitment records for unsuccessful applicants: 6 months after the decision.
  • Employment records: for the length of employment plus 6 years.
  • Payroll and tax records: 6 years after the tax year they relate to.
  • Training and acknowledgement records: for the length of employment plus 6 years.
  • Health and safety accident records: 3 years from the incident, or longer where the law requires.

The full schedule is in the Data Retention Schedule.

9. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and ask for it in a portable form. Ask [privacy email]; we answer within one month. Where a decision about you is made solely by automated means with legal or similar effect, you can ask for a person to review it. [We do not make such decisions. / We do so for: X.]

10. Complaints

Raise concerns with [Data Protection Lead] first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

11. Changes

We will tell you about material changes and ask you to acknowledge the new version through the Attest AI portal.

How to roll this out
  1. Replace every [bracketed] field. None may remain.
  2. Have your data protection lead and one HR reader check it against what you actually do.
  3. Set it live on the Governance dashboard; every seated member of staff is then asked to acknowledge it in their portal.
  4. Review annually and whenever a new tool, supplier or purpose is introduced.
