Data Protection Policy.

The rules every member of staff follows when they handle personal data, and who is accountable for what. Short enough to be read, specific enough to be enforced. Aligns with UK GDPR Article 5 and 24, DPA 2018, and ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.34.

Designed to print clean. Replace every bracketed field. Staff acknowledge this document from their portal once it is live.
Policy statement

Data Protection Policy of [Company Name]

Effective: [Date] · Version: 1.0 · Owner: [Role, e.g. Data Protection Lead / HR Director] · Review cycle: Annual, or on any change in processing.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how [Company Name] protects the personal data it holds about staff, clients, suppliers and anyone else. It applies to everyone who works for us, on any device, in any system, including AI tools.

2. The principles we follow

  1. Lawful, fair and transparent. We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis recorded in the Lawful Basis Register, and we tell people what we do in a privacy notice.
  2. Purpose limitation. Data collected for one purpose is not reused for another without checking the basis.
  3. Data minimisation. We hold what the purpose needs, no more.
  4. Accuracy. We correct data when told it is wrong.
  5. Storage limitation. We keep data for the periods in the Data Retention Schedule, then delete it.
  6. Integrity and confidentiality. We protect data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access with the measures in section 6.
  7. Accountability. We keep the records in section 8 that show we do the above.

3. Roles

  • [Board / Owner / Managing Director] is accountable for compliance and approves this policy.
  • Data Protection Lead: [name and role]. Maintains the registers, handles rights requests and breaches, advises on new processing. [We are / are not] required to appoint a statutory DPO under Article 37.
  • Line managers ensure their teams follow this policy and complete training.
  • Every member of staff follows the rules in section 5 and reports concerns without delay.

4. Special category and criminal offence data

Health, ethnicity, religion, trade union membership, sexual orientation, biometric and genetic data, and criminal records are processed only where an Article 9 or 10 condition applies, recorded in the Lawful Basis Register, with the Appropriate Policy Document required by DPA 2018 Schedule 1.

5. Rules for staff

  • Use company systems for personal data; not personal email, personal cloud storage or personal messaging.
  • Do not paste personal data into AI tools other than those approved in the Acceptable Use Policy, and never into free consumer tiers.
  • Share data on a need-to-know basis. Check the recipient before sending; use the correct email address and blind copy for group messages to outsiders.
  • Lock screens, use the password manager, enable multi-factor authentication where offered.
  • Do not take personal data off-site on removable media or paper unless approved.
  • Report a suspected breach or a rights request to [privacy email] the same day.

6. Security measures

Access control by role; multi-factor authentication on core systems; encryption in transit and at rest for [systems]; backups tested [quarterly]; supplier assessment before any new processor is engaged; secure disposal of devices and paper. Detail lives in [the information security policy].

7. Individual rights

Requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection go to the Data Protection Lead and are answered within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests. The Subject Access Request Procedure sets out the steps. Identity is verified before any data is released.

8. Records we keep

  • Records of Processing Activities (Article 30).
  • Lawful Basis and Legitimate Interests Register, with balancing tests.
  • Data Retention Schedule.
  • Data Protection Impact Assessments for high-risk processing, including new AI uses.
  • Breach log, including breaches not reported to the ICO and why.
  • Training and policy acknowledgement records (through the Attest AI platform).

9. Breaches

Any loss, theft, unauthorised access or disclosure of personal data is reported internally at once. The Data Protection Lead assesses risk and, where required, notifies the ICO within 72 hours and affected people without undue delay, following the Personal Data Breach Response Plan.

10. Suppliers and transfers

Processors are engaged only under a written contract meeting Article 28. Transfers outside the UK use adequacy regulations, the UK IDTA or the Addendum to EU SCCs, with a transfer risk assessment.

11. Training and acknowledgement

All staff complete data protection training at induction and annually, and acknowledge this policy through their portal. Breach of this policy is a disciplinary matter.
